# Email patterns

## Request with a deadline
Subject: Input needed on [topic] by [day, date]

Hi [Name],

Could you [specific request] by [day, time]? [One sentence of context or why it matters.]

If it is easier, [offer a shortcut, e.g. "a quick reply with a yes/no is enough"].

Thanks,
[Your name]

## Follow-up after no reply
Subject: Following up: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Following up on my note from [date] about [topic]. To move forward I need [decision/info] by [date]. Happy to talk for 10 minutes if that is quicker.

Best,
[Your name]

## Polite decline
Subject: Re: [subject]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for thinking of me. I cannot [do X] because [short reason]. [Alternative: someone else, later date, smaller scope.]

Best wishes,
[Your name]

## Complaint
Subject: [Order/Case #] problem with [item/service]

Dear [Team],

On [date] I [what happened]. The result was [impact]. I would like [specific fix: refund, replacement, correction] by [date]. [Attach evidence.]

Regards,
[Your name, contact]

## Apology
Subject: Apologies for [issue]

Hi [Name],

I am sorry for [specific mistake] and the [impact] it caused. It happened because [one-line cause]. I have [fix], and from now on [change]. Please let me know if anything is still outstanding.

[Your name]

## Introduction
Subject: Intro: [A] and [B]

Hi [A], meet [B], who [one-line credential and why relevant]. [B], [A] is [credential]. I think you could help each other with [topic]. I'll let you take it from here.

[Your name]
